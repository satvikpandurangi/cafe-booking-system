## Design
- Modern cafe aesthetic.
- Clean typography.
- Clear price hierarchy.
- Large touch targets.
- Responsive layout.
- Accessible contrast and focus states.
- Status badges.
- Loading, empty, success, and error states.

Customer UI should minimize taps to complete an order.
